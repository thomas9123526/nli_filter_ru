Russia-side policy flow

How nli_label.py applies policy/russia_llm_policy_v2.json to one document: where the markers, the gate, Level 1, Level 2, reports_or_debunks and the exceptions act, and which action each step can give.

Document Keyword scan

Looks for the words in keywords/russia_v3.txt + keywords/ukraine.txt, plus the marker terms. Cheap: no model runs here.

→no hit: document untouched, copied as it is
a hit

Document Split into paragraphs

Blocks of 200–2,000 characters. Only paragraphs with a keyword hit or a marker term go on; every other paragraph stays as it is.

→paragraphs without a hit are not labelled
each paragraph with a hit

Paragraph · L0 Gate: is it about Russia / Ukraine?

The paragraph gets through if any one of these is true:

  1. Marker term in the paragraph: orcs, rashists, рашисты, орки, кацапы, москали, хохлы, укропы, укры. Opens the gate without asking a model.
  2. Gate label involves_russia_ukraine ≥ 0.3: “This text mentions Russia, Ukraine, Russians or Ukrainians.”
  3. A Level-2 remove label (hostility_*, violence_call) is already positive (narrative_opens_gate: true). Hate is never let through just because the gate model said no.
→none of the three: paragraph pass (the keyword was incidental)
↓a pass paragraph still goes to the exception step, because ambiguous_our_country has scope keyword
open: Level 1 and Level 2 both run (run_if: gate or @marker)

Paragraph · L1 ∥ L2 Subjects and framing, scored independently

Neither level waits for the other or depends on its result. Each label is scored by ModernBERT; a score in the uncertain band 0.6–0.95 is checked again by the DeBERTa judge. A label is positive when its score reaches its threshold.

Paragraph Softener: reports_or_debunks

“This text reports or refutes what someone else claimed.” If it is positive (≥ 0.6), it softens hostility_* and violence_call: they count as review instead of remove. A news report quoting an insult is not the insult itself.

The softener has no action of its own; it only changes those labels.

Without a reports_or_debunks block in the policy, this step is skipped: every positive hostility or violence label is remove, news reports and fact-checks included.

→not positive: hostility / violence stay remove
→no such block in the policy: the same, for every paragraph
decide

Paragraph Decision rules, first match wins

  1. A Level-2 remove label is positive and not softened → remove. It beats every Level-1 keep (war, economy, culture).
  2. A Level-1 remove label and a keep label (L1 or L2) are both positive → conflict = review. Level-2 removes never reach this rule: rule 1 already decided them, or the softener made them review. This policy has no Level-1 remove label, so this rule never fires.
  3. Any review label is positive (history, politics, opinions, NATO claim, a softened hostility) → review.
  4. A keep label is positive → keep.
  5. Nothing positive, but it is about Russia / Ukraine → the policy's "no_subject", here keep (plain facts). It can be set to review or remove; the reason stays no_subject.
→one of remove review keep
result, or pass from the gate

Paragraph Exceptions (highest priority first)

ambiguous_our_country · priority 100 · scope keyword · skip_quoted: the paragraph contains “our country”, “our homeland”, “наша страна”, “наша країна”, … outside quotation marks → set to review (it is unclear whose country “our” is).

Applies to pass, keep and review results. A remove by a protected label is never lowered (recorded as exception_blocked).

→no phrase, or only inside quotes: result unchanged
every paragraph's final action

Document Document action and clean copy

  1. A paragraph is remove (or review with --review-as remove) → it is cut: the document is edited, or removed if fewer than 50 characters are left.
  2. Nothing cut, a paragraph is review → review.
  3. Every labelled paragraph is pass → pass; otherwise kept.

The clean copy (--write-clean) then rewrites place names to the Russian spelling (Kyiv → Kiev), from policy/place_names_ru.csv.

→<file>.labels.jsonl.zst, .clean.jsonl.zst, .rewrites.jsonl.zst

The traces show the intended path; real scores can differ (the “orcs” report paragraph was cut in the docs test because reports_or_debunks scored 0.47, below 0.6). Source: resolve() and apply_exceptions() in nli_label.py, policy/russia_llm_policy_v2.json as of 2026-11-23.
