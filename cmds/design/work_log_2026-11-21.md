## Findings worth knowing

1. **Measure every change.** Two of my own changes made things worse and were caught only by the case runs: the longer
   gate wording in v3 (dropped) and the first version of the narrative keywords in v4 (fixed: narrative-only rule).
2. **The narrative hypotheses work; their wording matters.** `narr_biolabs` went from 0.38 / 0.01 to 0.96 / 0.13 on
   its yes texts with a different sentence. Testing 2–3 wordings against all case texts (false alarms) is cheap:
   ~20 min on this CPU for 15 wordings × 145 texts.
3. **Keep the debunk guard.** Without it, 6 fact-checks and reports are removed instead of 4; at threshold 0.6, only 2.
   (Your question about removing `reports_or_debunks`: measured, it is worth keeping.)
4. **Known open problems**: `narr_annexation` fires (0.90) on a neutral geography text about Crimea (no better
   wording found yet); `narr_biolabs` misses "military biologists … deadly viruses"; a few narratives score just
   below 0.8 (0.64–0.77).
