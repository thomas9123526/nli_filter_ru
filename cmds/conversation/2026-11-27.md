## Q: "Positive, but no positive label to soften." — seen at the Softener step on the Experiment page. What does it mean?

The softener label matched but had nothing to soften, so it changed nothing.

- The softener in `russia_llm_policy_v2.json` is `reports_or_debunks` (level 2, threshold 0.9, "reports or refutes what
  someone else said"), with `softens: ["hostility_*", "violence_call"]`: when it is positive, those labels give
  **review** instead of **remove** (a paragraph that only reports hostile words is not removed as hostile).
- The step's three messages:
  1. "X is positive: … count as review instead of remove": the softener and a label it softens are both positive.
  2. **"Positive, but no positive label to soften."**: the softener scored ≥ 0.9, but none of hostility_russians,
     hostility_ukrainians, violence_call reached its threshold. No removal to lower; the decision is unaffected.
  3. "Not positive: nothing is softened.": the softener is below 0.9.
- A positive softener does not make the paragraph "keep": a label with `softens` only lowers other labels' removals,
  its own decision is ignored (`nli_label.py`, `decide`).
- "protected" (hostility_*, violence_call, criticize_about_something) is a different rule: a removal by them is never
  lowered by an *exception* (the policy's exceptions list). The softener is not an exception, so it can still lower
  them to review.
