# 2026-11-26

## Q: The Hunyuan-MT licence note in `cmds/viewer/translate.md` excludes some regions (the EU, the UK and others). Does this mean I cannot translate into those regions' languages?

No. The Territory is about **where** the model is used, not which languages it translates.

- **Languages:** translating into German, French or English is fine, as long as the model and its translations are
  used outside the excluded regions.
- **Places:** running the model, or using its translations, inside an excluded region is not licensed, even for
  Chinese or Russian. Examples: a server in Germany, or a colleague in London using the viewer.
- **For this project:** running it here and reading Chinese / Russian translations in the viewer is fine. The other
  limit is the one that matters more: never put its translations into training data, label files or test sets.

This is a plain reading of the licence, not legal advice. If the project or its data ever moves into those regions, ask
whoever handles licences, or use Seed-X-PPO-7B instead.
